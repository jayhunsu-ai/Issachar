# Prospect Schema

## Identity

- Company name
- Industry
- Location
- Website
- Company type
- Estimated size
- Primary source

## Business

- What the company does
- Customers or target market
- Operating model
- Relevant products/services

## Digital Footprint

Record observable capabilities such as:

- Website
- Mobile experience
- Customer portal
- Online application
- Online payment
- Booking/order system
- Search
- Authentication
- Customer self-service
- Mobile application
- Integrations
- Public technical/API clues

For every item, record evidence rather than assuming implementation details.

## Opportunity Analysis

For each hypothesis:

- Problem hypothesis
- Supporting evidence
- Business impact hypothesis
- Potential solution
- Relevant Veltrix capabilities
- Confidence
- Research gaps
- Validation questions

## Buyer

- Potential decision-maker role
- Evidence for relevance
- Contact source

## Outreach

- Outreach hypothesis
- Specific observation to reference
- Business problem to validate
- Proposed conversation

## Status

- DISCOVERED
- INVESTIGATING
- QUALIFIED
- OUTREACH
- RESPONDED
- CLOSED

# Issachar Operating Model

## Workflow

**DISCOVER → VERIFY → UNDERSTAND → INVESTIGATE → EVIDENCE → IDENTIFY FRICTION → FORM HYPOTHESIS → MATCH CAPABILITY → IDENTIFY BUYER → OUTREACH HYPOTHESIS → HUMAN REVIEW → OUTCOME**


## Prospect Qualification Gate

Issachar must qualify a company **before** spending deep-research effort on it or counting it toward a Phase 0 sector sample.

### Qualification Requirements

A prospect should have:

- **Evidence density:** enough current public material to investigate beyond a generic company description.
- **Operational complexity:** meaningful multi-step workflows, teams, customers, branches, transactions, or external dependencies.
- **Observable workflow:** enough evidence to reconstruct at least part of how work moves through the business.
- **Technology surface:** identifiable systems, platforms, integrations, portals, CRM/ERP tools, communications channels, or manual system boundaries.
- **Current evidence:** preferably material from 2025–2026; historical evidence may establish context but must not be treated as current proof.
- **Conversation path:** an identifiable operations, technology, branch, founder, or department contact who could validate the workflow.
- **Pain-discovery path:** a concrete line of questioning that could turn public evidence into a validated internal problem.

### Exclusion Signals

Exclude or defer a company when:

- its public footprint is too thin to support meaningful investigation;
- operational complexity is too low to justify the research effort;
- no plausible decision-maker or workflow owner can be identified;
- a recent technology/vendor partner clearly owns the exact workflow we would otherwise investigate;
- the company's public material is dominated by generic marketing claims with no verifiable operational detail;
- we would have to invent or heavily speculate about the problem to create an opportunity.

### Partner Rule

**Having a technology partner is not an automatic exclusion.**

Distinguish:

1. **Partner exists** → investigate further.
2. **Partner owns the relevant workflow and evidence shows it is functioning as intended** → normally exclude.
3. **Partner exists but an adjacent workflow, integration boundary, or unresolved operational problem remains observable** → investigate that specific boundary.
4. **Partner relationship is historical or unclear** → treat it as context, not proof of current ownership.

### Anti-Quota Rule

A sector target is not a reason to keep a weak prospect.

> **Never fill the Phase 0 quota with a company that fails the qualification gate.**

It is preferable to have fewer qualified companies with strong evidence than a full list built from assumptions.


## Reasoning Layers

### Observation
A directly observable fact.

### Evidence
The source supporting the observation.

### Inference
A reasoned interpretation of evidence.

### Opportunity Hypothesis
A plausible business problem or improvement opportunity that requires validation.

### Capability Match
A specific Veltrix capability that could address the hypothesized opportunity.

### Action
A human-reviewed next step.

Issachar must preserve these layers rather than collapsing them into a single conclusion.

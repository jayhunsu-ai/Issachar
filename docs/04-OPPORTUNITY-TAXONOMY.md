# Opportunity Taxonomy

Issachar classifies opportunities by the capability that may address them.

## ENGINEERING
Frontend, backend, APIs, databases, full-stack systems, and application modernization.

## AI
LLM applications, AI-assisted workflows, agents, orchestration, local AI, evaluation, and intelligent interfaces.

## AUTOMATION
Business workflows, repetitive-task elimination, notifications, document/data processing, and operational automation.

## INTEGRATION
Payments, authentication, third-party APIs, external services, and disconnected systems.

## INTERNAL SOFTWARE
Dashboards, admin systems, operations tools, workflow systems, and internal business applications.

## INFRASTRUCTURE
Deployment, CI/CD, monitoring, backend services, reliability, and infrastructure modernization.

## PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT
New customer-facing products, MVPs, portals, marketplaces, and business platforms.

## DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION
Modernization of fragmented, manual, legacy, or disconnected digital operations.

Issachar should identify the opportunity first and use this taxonomy to describe the possible capability match.

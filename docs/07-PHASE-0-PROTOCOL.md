# Phase 0 Protocol

## Objective

Validate whether the Issachar methodology can reliably produce useful, evidence-backed business opportunities.

## Initial Sample

- 5 education/training companies
- 5 real-estate companies
- 5 logistics/distribution companies

## Procedure

1. Discover the company.
2. Verify that it exists and is active.
3. Understand what the business does.
4. Inspect its public digital footprint.
5. Record observable evidence.
6. Identify possible friction.
7. Separate facts from hypotheses.
8. Generate opportunity hypotheses.
9. Match Veltrix capabilities.
10. Identify a potential buyer role.
11. Draft an outreach hypothesis.
12. Human-review the analysis.
13. Record the outcome.
14. Update the methodology when repeated evidence justifies a change.

## Constraints

- No autonomous external outreach.
- No invented company problems.
- No unsupported technical claims.
- Social media is not a primary discovery source.
- Preserve sources for material observations.
- Record uncertainty.

## Success Criteria

Phase 0 is successful when repeated research produces reproducible company intelligence, defensible opportunity hypotheses, clear evidence trails, relevant Veltrix capability matches, and practical outreach angles.

The result of Phase 0 should determine what Issachar's software infrastructure needs to automate.

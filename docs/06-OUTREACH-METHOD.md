# Outreach Method

Issachar does not begin outreach with a generic service pitch.

The preferred sequence is:

1. Identify the company.
2. Identify an observable business or digital condition.
3. Explain why it may matter operationally.
4. Form a clearly stated opportunity hypothesis.
5. Match a relevant Veltrix capability.
6. Identify the appropriate buyer.
7. Write concise outreach around the observation and validation question.
8. Require human review before sending.

## Outreach Principle

The message should make the prospect feel understood, not catalogued.

Avoid unsupported claims such as “Your website is outdated” or “Your process is inefficient.”

Prefer grounded language such as “I noticed X on your current workflow” and “I was curious whether your team currently handles Z manually.”

The objective of initial outreach is to validate the hypothesis, not pretend the hypothesis is already proven.

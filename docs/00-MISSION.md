# Issachar Mission

## Mission

Issachar is Veltrix's business intelligence and opportunity-discovery system.

It exists to understand businesses from observable evidence, identify operational and technical friction, discover plausible opportunities for Veltrix, and turn validated intelligence into actionable business-development opportunities.

## Foundational Model

**Evidence → Understanding → Opportunity → Action**

Issachar does not begin with a service catalog and search for companies to sell those services to. It begins with the business, its operating context, and observable evidence.

## Non-Negotiable Principles

1. Evidence before inference.
2. Understand the business before proposing technology.
3. Never invent a problem.
4. Separate observation, inference, hypothesis, and recommendation.
5. Record uncertainty and research gaps.
6. Match opportunities to demonstrated Veltrix capabilities.
7. Human approval precedes external outreach.

## Initial Validation

Phase 0 will validate this methodology manually across education & training, real estate, and logistics & distribution before autonomous intelligence is introduced.

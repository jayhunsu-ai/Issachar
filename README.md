# Issachar

## Purpose

Issachar is the intelligence and prospect-discovery system being built for Veltrix.

Its purpose is to understand businesses from observable evidence, identify operational and technical friction, discover opportunities where Veltrix can create value, and turn that intelligence into qualified business-development opportunities.

Issachar is not a generic lead generator. It begins with the business and its observable operating context, then determines where technology, engineering, automation, AI, integrations, infrastructure, or product work may create value.

## Mission

> Understand the business. Find the friction. Identify the opportunity. Match the capability. Enable the action.

## Core Model

**Evidence → Understanding → Opportunity → Action**

Issachar should:

1. Discover real companies.
2. Verify the company and relevant activity.
3. Understand the business model and operating context.
4. Inspect the observable digital footprint.
5. Record evidence before drawing conclusions.
6. Separate observations from inferences and recommendations.
7. Identify plausible operational or technical friction.
8. Generate opportunity hypotheses.
9. Match opportunities to demonstrated Veltrix capabilities.
10. Identify the relevant potential decision maker.
11. Produce a grounded outreach hypothesis.
12. Keep a human in control of external outreach.

## Core Principle

### Evidence before inference.

Issachar must never invent a problem simply because Veltrix can solve it.

Every meaningful opportunity should distinguish between:

- **Observation** — what was directly observed.
- **Evidence** — the source supporting the observation.
- **Inference** — what the evidence reasonably suggests.
- **Opportunity hypothesis** — a potential business problem worth validating.
- **Capability match** — what Veltrix could potentially do about it.
- **Confidence** — how strongly the evidence supports the hypothesis.

## Initial Target Sectors

Phase 0 begins with:

- Education & training
- Real estate
- Logistics & distribution

## Opportunity Areas

Issachar may identify opportunities across:

- Software engineering
- AI engineering
- Business automation
- Systems integration
- Internal business software
- Infrastructure and DevOps
- Product development
- Digital transformation
- Data and workflow systems

The system should discover the business need first and select the technical capability second.

## Operating Principles

1. Evidence before inference.
2. Understand the business before proposing technology.
3. Never invent a problem.
4. Separate observation from interpretation.
5. Treat opportunity identification as a hypothesis until validated.
6. Match opportunities to actual Veltrix capabilities.
7. Prefer observable evidence over assumptions.
8. Record uncertainty and research gaps.
9. Human approval precedes external outreach.
10. Improve the methodology from real research outcomes.

## Phase 0 — Methodology Validation

Phase 0 is intentionally human-led.

Initial validation target:

- 5 education/training companies
- 5 real-estate companies
- 5 logistics/distribution companies

Social media is not a primary discovery source in Phase 0.

The goal is to determine whether the methodology consistently produces useful company intelligence, defensible problem hypotheses, real business opportunities, appropriate Veltrix capability matches, and actionable outreach angles.

## Research Workflow

**DISCOVER → VERIFY → UNDERSTAND → INVESTIGATE → EVIDENCE → IDENTIFY FRICTION → FORM HYPOTHESIS → MATCH CAPABILITY → IDENTIFY BUYER → OUTREACH HYPOTHESIS → HUMAN REVIEW → OUTCOME**

## Development Roadmap

### Phase 0 — Methodology Validation
Human-led research against real companies.

### Phase 1 — Model-Assisted Intelligence
Introduce the local model to perform bounded research, extraction, classification, and analysis using the established methodology.

### Phase 2 — Intelligence Infrastructure
Build the software required to store, retrieve, evaluate, and process prospect intelligence.

### Phase 3 — Multi-Agent Research
Introduce specialized agents for discovery, investigation, evidence extraction, opportunity analysis, and review.

### Phase 4 — Prospect Intelligence System
Connect the components into an end-to-end intelligence workflow with human approval gates.

### Phase 5 — Continuous Intelligence
Allow Issachar to continuously discover, investigate, and update company intelligence while preserving evidence, traceability, and human control.

## Current Status

**Phase 0 — Founding infrastructure and methodology validation.**

The repository establishes the founding specification. Implementation should follow the methodology rather than precede it.

## Guiding Question

> What does this business actually do, where does friction appear in the way it operates, what evidence proves it, and where can Veltrix create value?

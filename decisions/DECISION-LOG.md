# Decision Log

## DEC-001 — Evidence-driven prospect intelligence

**Decision:** Issachar will identify business opportunities from evidence about how companies operate rather than starting from a list of services to sell.

**Reason:** The system is intended to discover needs a company may not explicitly advertise or recognize.

**Status:** Accepted

## DEC-002 — Human-led Phase 0

**Decision:** Methodology validation will happen manually before autonomous model-driven prospecting is introduced.

**Reason:** The system needs a validated reasoning framework and evidence standard before automation.

**Status:** Accepted

## DEC-003 — Social media excluded from primary Phase 0 discovery

**Decision:** Social media will not be a primary discovery source during Phase 0.

**Reason:** Phase 0 should prioritize sources that are easier to revisit, verify, and preserve as evidence.

**Status:** Accepted

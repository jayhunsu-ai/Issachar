## Qualification

PWAN Max passes the evidence gate because there is both a substantial operating footprint and a company-specific regulatory record concerning customer allocation/documentation. Its public site describes property marketing, investment, management and development, while LinkedIn lists 201–500 employees. citeturn2search1turn2search14

## Evidence of Operational Friction

On 9 June 2026, the FCCPC reported sealing PWAN Max's operational premises after a complaint concerning 20 fully-paid plots that allegedly had not been allocated despite repeated directives and compliance notices. The FCCPC said the matter began with a February 2025 consumer petition and that PWAN Max had undertaken to allocate the plots and provide title documents by 30 June 2025 but allegedly failed to comply. citeturn1search3

This is company-specific evidence, not an industry-level hypothesis.

## Observable Workflow Surface

The documented case exposes a concrete workflow boundary:

**customer payment → subscription/plot allocation → title/document delivery → compliance follow-up → customer/regulator communication**

PWAN Max also publicly describes due-diligence and documentation processes for its properties. citeturn2search1

## Technology / Opportunity Hypothesis

The public evidence does **not** prove that software caused the allocation failure.

A testable technology hypothesis is whether allocation status, payment state, title-document state, internal ownership and compliance deadlines are maintained as one reliable case record, with escalations when commitments become overdue.

Potential Veltrix capabilities:
- workflow/state tracking
- document-status management
- deadline/escalation automation
- customer-facing status visibility
- operational dashboards and audit trails

## Partner / Vendor Check

No technology vendor was identified in the sources reviewed as owning this exact allocation/compliance workflow. PWAN Max is part of the broader PWAN ecosystem; that relationship is corporate affiliation, not evidence of a workflow vendor. citeturn2search11

## Why It Is Stored

Unlike 1159 Realty, this prospect has externally documented company-specific operational friction. A conversation is useful for validating the internal workflow and identifying whether technology is an actionable lever, but the existence of the underlying customer/process failure does not depend on getting a conversation first.

## Qualification

DNJ has a long-running development footprint and a current public portfolio. Its official site says the company has developed infrastructure and real-estate projects since 1983 and lists 19+ completed projects. citeturn2search21

## Evidence of Operational Friction

A July 2026 report on a Lagos High Court case states that a subscriber sued DNJ Properties & Investment Ltd after paying ₦50 million for a two-bedroom apartment. The subscriber alleged that the 18-month delivery period expired on 29 May 2025 without delivery and that construction had not begun as of the relevant point in the dispute. The court dismissed DNJ's preliminary objection and adjourned the substantive matter for further hearing. These are allegations in ongoing litigation, not a final finding that DNJ breached the contract. citeturn2search21turn0search0

## Observable Workflow Surface

The case exposes a concrete workflow:

**subscription/payment → development/project status → promised delivery date → customer communication → contractual escalation**

The company's own site confirms that its business includes real-estate development and project delivery. citeturn2search21

## Technology / Opportunity Hypothesis

The evidence does not establish that DNJ's delivery problem was caused by software.

A testable opportunity is whether project milestones, subscriber commitments, payment state, allocation information and customer communications are tracked in a system that makes overdue delivery commitments visible early.

Potential Veltrix capabilities:
- project/subscriber state tracking
- milestone and deadline monitoring
- document and commitment records
- escalation workflows
- customer communication/status dashboards
- audit trails for changes and commitments

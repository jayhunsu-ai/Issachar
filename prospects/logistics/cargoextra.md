## Qualification

CargoExtra has a substantial observable technology and operations surface: international freight, warehousing, customs clearing, doorstep delivery, procurement, shipment tracking, rate calculation and multiple operating locations. ## Evidence of Operational Friction

The Nigerian Shippers' Council reported in May 2026 that it mediated a complaint by Adelheid Nigeria Limited against Cargoextra Logistics concerning an LCL shipment. The dispute involved a claimed discrepancy around the shipment's measured CBM and the final billable quantity. The Council's case record identifies Cargoextra as the logistics company involved and documents a formal dispute-resolution process. citeturn0search7

This is company-specific operational evidence involving shipment documentation, measurement and billing — not merely a generic logistics problem.

## Technology / Opportunity Hypothesis

The evidence does not prove that CargoExtra's systems caused the CBM dispute.

A testable opportunity is whether shipment dimensions, weight/CBM calculations, booking data, invoices and customer-facing quotes remain synchronized across the shipment lifecycle, with a clear audit trail when measurements or charges change.

Potential Veltrix capabilities:
- workflow/state orchestration
- calculation and validation services
- shipment/document integration
- billing/quote reconciliation
- audit trails
- exception dashboards

## Partner / Vendor Check

CargoExtra explicitly says it works with airlines and global freight-forwarding partners. Those are operating partners, not evidence that an external vendor owns the company's shipment/quote workflow. citeturn3search4turn3search3

## Why It Is Stored

CargoExtra survives the evidence gate because the operational friction is independently documented and maps to a concrete digital workflow. We do not need a conversation to establish that a shipment/billing dispute occurred; the conversation is for determining recurrence, internal process, and whether Veltrix can materially improve the boundary.

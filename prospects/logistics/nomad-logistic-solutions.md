- e-commerce inventory, packing and delivery;

## Partner / Vendor Check

Nomad publicly advertises fleet, rider, investor and international/forwarder partnerships. These are operational/network relationships, not evidence that an external technology vendor owns Nomad's core workflow. citeturn1search1

No current public source reviewed for this qualification pass identifies a named technology vendor that clearly owns the core dispatch/reconciliation workflow.

**Important:** Partner relationships do not automatically disqualify Nomad. We need to determine whether partner handoffs create an unresolved operational boundary.

## Investigation Hypotheses

These are **not established problems**:

1. Whether payment-on-delivery reconciliation remains fully synchronized across riders, deliveries and bank remittance.
2. Whether long-haul partner handoffs create status/reconciliation gaps.
3. Whether control-room staff manually reconcile information across dispatch, customer support and partner channels.
4. Whether recurring B2B deliveries require manual scheduling or exception handling outside the core dispatch system.

## Evidence Gaps

We still need direct evidence of:
- recurring dispatch/reconciliation friction;
- manual exception handling;
- delayed or inconsistent status updates;
- partner handoff failures;
- payment discrepancies;
- measurable operational impact.

**Do not pitch or update this prospect as having a confirmed pain point until one of these is evidenced.**

**Observation:** A verified Clutch review from AIMS Education's Marketing Manager describes an Adroit Infosystem engagement beginning in January 2021 and listed as ongoing at the time of the review.

The stated objective was to streamline and automate internal operations and develop an in-depth Analytics/MIS system. Deliverables included:
- software integration with the website, Facebook and WhatsApp;
- a lead-tracking module from inquiry to enrollment;
- workflows for follow-ups, reminders and document submissions;
- simplification/automation of complicated invoicing;
- transparency between stakeholders. citeturn0search4turn3search0

AIMS reported:
- 20% reduction in processing time for key operations;
- 25% reduction in data-entry/processing errors;
- more accessible/up-to-date records;
- increased cash flow and lower operational costs associated with the AR/automation modules. citeturn0search4turn3search0

**Important temporal limitation:** The review was published in September 2024. It is evidence of a real AIMS technology requirement and reported implementation results, but does not prove that the same implementation or architecture remains unchanged in October 2026.

## Opportunity Hypotheses

These are hypotheses only and are NOT established business problems.

### H1 — Post-implementation integration / modernization

The historical automation system may have evolved unevenly as the business, website, offices and service scope changed.

**Potential capabilities:** Integration, backend/API engineering, internal software, digital transformation.

**Confidence:** Low–Medium.

### H2 — End-to-end student lifecycle visibility

The documented automation project explicitly tracked inquiry → enrollment. Aims' current public service scope extends beyond that into applications, visa/compliance, pre-departure and post-arrival support. citeturn0search4turn0search16

**Question:** Does the current system provide a unified lifecycle view from lead through final student outcome?

**Potential capabilities:** Internal software, dashboards, APIs, automation.

**Confidence:** Low–Medium.

### H3 — Website/CRM lead synchronization

The current website collects structured leads while Aims relies on CRM-based student/application management.

**Question:** Are web leads automatically normalized, deduplicated, assigned and tracked in the CRM?

**Potential capabilities:** APIs, integrations, workflow automation.

**Confidence:** Low.

### H4 — Deadline/document workflow automation

Aims publicly describes handling application deadlines, payment deadlines, interview dates and document workflows.

**Question:** Are reminders, escalations and document-state transitions fully automated across all active applicants?

**Potential capabilities:** Automation, notifications, internal dashboards.

**Confidence:** Low–Medium.

### H5 — Course-catalogue/application-system synchronization

The current public site has a searchable course catalogue with country/destination/subject/study-level filtering.

**Question:** Is course data synchronized with application workflows, partner-university data and counsellor-facing records, or maintained as a separate content/data system?

**Potential capabilities:** APIs, data integration, internal tools, search infrastructure.

**Confidence:** Low.

## Current Assessment

Aims Education is a **strong Phase 0 investigation candidate** because there is unusually strong public evidence of an existing technology-dependent operating model, a documented history of purchasing operational automation, and active 2026 expansion/repositioning.

The most important new finding is that the historical AIMS/Adroit requirements closely overlap with Adroit's current Study CRM product capabilities. This makes the **current system identity and post-implementation evolution** the highest-value research target.

However, **no current 2026 business problem has been proven yet**.

The correct next step is not a generic software pitch. It is to determine whether the existing CRM/MIS/website/course/application stack has remained coherent as Aims expanded its offices, partner network, course catalogue and service lifecycle.

**Core Issachar question:** What changed in Aims' operating system between the documented automation implementation and its current 2026 multi-office, multi-service operation—and is there evidence of a new unresolved constraint?

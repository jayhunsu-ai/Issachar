# Detailed prospect notes — ranks 1–15
Research date: 17 October 2026. A vacancy or KPI is a discovery signal, not proof of failure.

## 1. APM Terminals Apapa — 94/100
- **Signal:** H1 2026 exports rose 6%; the terminal publicly described export-delay root-cause work and planned 24-hour barge operations in Q4. This suggests discovery around hand-offs among export documentation, barge slots, gate access and third-party exceptions—not a proven platform failure.
- **Buyer:** Terminal Operations Director, Export Operations, Commercial/Customer Experience.
- **Route:** [Official terminal contacts](https://www.apmterminals.com/en/apapa/contact/contacts-and-directions); ask for Export Operations/Process Improvement.
- **Veltrix fit:** Container-level exception queue with delay reason, owner, ageing, next action and closure evidence.
- **Urgency / buying:** High / medium-high; public process work and Q4 change are timely, enterprise procurement is hard.
- **Confidence:** Very high for signal, medium-high for fit.
- **Rabbit holes:** Gate dwell, barge-slot variance, documentation holds.
- **Sources:** [Export growth](https://www2.apmterminals.com/en/apapa/about/news-and-updates/2026/260901-APMTerminals-Apapa-records-6pc-export-growth); [barge/delay work](https://akamai.apmterminals.com/en/apapa/about/news-and-updates/2026/260914-APM-Terminals-Apapa-Strengthens-Exports-with-24-Hour-Barge-Operations).

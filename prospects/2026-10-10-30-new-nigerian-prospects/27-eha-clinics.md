# 27. EHA Clinics

- **Industry:** Multi-site healthcare
- **Priority:** Medium-high
- **Evidence confidence:** Strong multi-site/scale-up evidence; medium gap inference
- **Likely decision-maker:** COO / Clinic Operations / Medical Operations
- **Best outreach route:** info@eha.ng
- **Official site/source route:** https://www.eha.ng/

## Activity / trigger evidence
Official site lists clinics in Abuja, Kano and Lagos, digital health services and selection for Cascador ScaleUp Program in 2026.

## Specific operational hypothesis to validate
Referral, diagnostic status and follow-up actions may be difficult to track when care spans services or locations.

## Realistic Veltrix offer
Referral/appointment workflow, diagnostic-status dashboard and employer-client reporting.

## Buying likelihood and urgency
**Medium-high.** Qualitative estimate based on the operational signal and likely value of improving the named workflow; not evidence of an approved budget or intent to buy.

## Evidence strength and caveats
Strong multi-site/scale-up evidence; medium gap inference. Where the evidence is a vacancy or expansion announcement, it demonstrates operating priorities or change—not necessarily system failure. Ask a discovery question before making claims.

## First discovery question
“What is the hardest part of keeping multi-site healthcare operations visible across the teams/locations involved, and how do you currently track exceptions or overdue actions?”

## Rabbit holes / follow-up research
- Confirm the cited vacancy/announcement and its date from the original source before outreach.
- Identify the named functional owner and verify the contact route from an official company channel.
- Ask whether the workflow is currently managed in spreadsheets, email, an ERP, or a specialist platform before pitching implementation.

## Outreach rules

- Lead with the specific observed trigger, then ask a short diagnostic question. Never assert that a company has a broken system unless a source proves it.
- Pitch Veltrix as a focused workflow/dashboard pilot, not a replacement for ERP, aviation safety systems, plant-control systems or other regulated core systems.
- Use only the official route where available. Recruitment and directory-listed email addresses are marked for verification.
- Buying likelihood is a qualitative prioritisation estimate, not a claim of known budget or intent.
- Verify no duplicates against the full Issachar repository and existing send queue before sending.

## Source handling

Each profile records the source URL available in the research notes. Some underlying vacancy/news claims were identified in the research run but their original article URLs were not retained in the summary; those are marked as source-follow-up items rather than presented as independently audited citations. Before outreach, reopen the referenced official site and independently locate the vacancy/announcement or downgrade the evidence.
